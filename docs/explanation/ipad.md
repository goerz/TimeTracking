# The iPad app

The iPad runs the same app as the iPhone, with the room to show everything the Mac shows. Its tabs follow the Mac's sidebar: Timer, Calendar, Reports, Projects, and Activity, and they can collapse into a sidebar.

The Timer tab is the [iPhone's screen](iphone.md), with the card and the list side by side on a wide screen. [Calendar](calendar.md), [Reports](reports.md), [Projects](projects.md), and [Activity](activity.md) behave as they do on the Mac, so those pages apply here too.

## Touch instead of a pointer

Mouse gestures map to touch. To draw an entry or a time block in the Calendar, long-press and then drag; an Apple Pencil draws directly. To move a card, long-press to lift it and drag. A selected card shows large resize handles. Where the Mac uses hover, a tap shows the same information, and a trackpad or Apple Pencil hover works as on the Mac. Right-click becomes a long press.

In the Calendar, pinch vertically to zoom the hours and horizontally to change the day width. A sideways swipe moves through time, and a swipe that starts mostly up or down scrolls the hours.

## With a keyboard

With a hardware keyboard, the iPad's menu bar has the Mac's menus and keys, and ⌘1 to ⌘5 switch tabs. There are small differences where the iPad has no counterpart: no Mini Timer, for example, so ⌘1 goes to the Timer tab with the cursor in the task field.

## What the iPad doesn't do

The menu bar item, global shortcuts, idle detection, the Mac's reminders, the command-line tool, and the tracker all stay on the Mac. In the Activity tab, the iPad reads the synced log and shows no open block, drill-down, or cause of away time. It can [collect its own activity](../howto/ios-activity.md), like the iPhone, and it asks for calendar access the first time the Calendar tab opens.

The [iPad section of the iOS spec](../reference/ios.md#10-the-ipad) has the complete mapping.
