# Set up clients, areas, and projects

If the terms are new, read [Clients, areas, projects, and tasks](../explanation/concepts.md) first. All of this happens in the Projects section (⌘4) under "Group by: Client and Area", where the whole structure is on screen.

## Create a project while tracking

Click the project chip in the Timer, or press ⌘J, type the name, and click "+ Create Project". The new project goes straight onto the running entry. In the task field, `@` followed by the name opens the same picker.

## Create a client

Choose File › New Client…. Enter a name and a color. The app creates the client together with its general area, which carries that color.

## Create an area

Choose File › New Area… (⇧⌥⌘N), or right-click a client and choose New Area…. Give it a name and a color. Areas of different clients may share a name. Use Move Up and Move Down in the context menu to set the order, which then applies everywhere.

## Recolor an area

Double-click its row, choose a color, and confirm. All entries of its projects change color at once, past ones included.

## Move a project

Double-click the project, then choose another Area or Client. Its whole history moves along. A move to another client asks for confirmation, since past reports will change.

## Finish a project

Select it and press ⌫ to archive it, or choose Project › Archive. A running entry on it keeps running. To bring it back, turn on "Include archived projects" in Filters, select it, and press ⌫ again.

## Merge duplicates

Right-click the project to give up and choose Merge Into…, then pick the project to keep. All entries move over and the first project is deleted. This can't be undone.

## Retire an area

Once all of an area's projects are archived, right-click it and choose Retire. It leaves the pickers and stays in your reports. Reactivate brings it back.

## Pin favorites for the widgets

On the iPhone or iPad, open Settings, Widgets, Pinned projects. The [Pinned widget](widgets.md) shows them.
