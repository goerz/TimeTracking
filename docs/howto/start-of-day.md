# Move the start of the day

On the Mac, open Settings, General, and choose an hour under "Start of day". On the iPhone or iPad, open Settings in the app. Pick an hour in the middle of your night, when you are least likely to be working, such as 04:00.

Every view rearranges at once, on all your devices, and no entry changes. Pick 00:00 to go back. [The start of day](../explanation/start-of-day.md) explains what follows it and what stays on the clock.
