# Handle time away from the Mac

## Answer the idle prompt

If an entry runs and you have been away for at least the idle threshold, ten minutes by default, the Mini Timer comes forward with "You have been away" when you return. It names when you left and offers four answers.

| Choose | When | Key |
|---|---|---|
| Split | You did something else: the entry stops when you left, and a new empty entry runs from that moment, ready for a task | ⌘S |
| Discard and Continue | The break doesn't count, and the work goes on: the entry stops when you left, and a new one with the same task starts now | ⇧⌘D |
| Discard | The break doesn't count, and you are done: the entry stops when you left | ⌘D |
| Keep | The time away was work: the entry runs on as if nothing happened | Return |

While the tracker runs, a video or a call counts as passive use, and no prompt appears.

## Change what counts as away

Open Settings, Idle. Set "Idle after" to anywhere from 1 to 120 minutes, or turn detection off. "On lock or sleep" decides what happens when the screen locks or the Mac sleeps: ask when you return, stop the entry at that moment, or keep tracking. "Stop the running entry on shutdown or logout" is on by default.

## Get reminded to start the timer

Open Settings, Reminders, and turn on "Remind me to track time". While nothing runs inside the hours and days you set, a notification arrives every N minutes, by default every 10 minutes from 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday. It offers Start New, Continue Latest, and Snooze.

## On the iPhone and iPad

These devices can't tell when you are away. Instead, Settings, Notifications has "Remind me when an entry runs long", which asks "Still tracking…?" after a number of hours you choose.
