# Turn activity into time entries

This needs the [tracker](tracker.md) to be running, so that the Calendar's Activity column has cards.

1. Press ⌘2 and go to the day. Bright parts of the gray cards are stretches no entry covers yet; covered parts are dimmed.
2. Click a card to see what it holds: its time, device, main workspace, and items.
3. Click "Create Entry…" in that popover. Alternatively, hover over the card and click its "+" button, drag the card into the Tracked column, or select it and press Space.
4. A provisional entry appears over the card's longest uncovered stretch. Enter the task and project, and press Return.

If the provisional times aren't quite right, keep the entry and drag its edges afterward.

To find a day's gaps from the other direction, use the [Activity section](../explanation/activity.md). Click a heatmap cell or a bar there, and the Calendar opens at that hour with the matching cards highlighted. ⌘5 takes you back.

To remove a stretch from the record altogether, right-click a finished card on the Mac and choose "Delete This Activity…". This removes it on every device and can't be undone; entries are not touched.
