# Work in Pomodoro phases

A Pomodoro lays timed focus and break phases over your running entry. It never starts, stops, or splits entries, so your tracked time is the same with or without it.

## Start a Pomodoro

Start an entry, then click the apple button at the left of the Timer bar, or press ⇧⌘P. The button widens into a capsule with a ring and a countdown. The elapsed time next to the task keeps showing the entry's duration.

## Move between phases

When a focus phase ends you get a notification, and the capsule turns into a "Break" button. Click it, press ⌘↩, or use the notification's Start Break action. The next phase doesn't start by itself: until you confirm, the menu bar counts the time since the end as overtime, such as `+1:20`.

To move on early, press ⌃⌘P, which always performs the next step, such as End Focus Now. Click the capsule for the same choices, including Skip Break and Cancel Pomodoro.

## Change the durations

Open Settings, Pomodoro. Focus defaults to 25 minutes and break to 5. A change applies from the next phase. The durations sync across your devices, and "Play a sound when a focus or break ends" is set per device.

To have the phase end interrupt you on the Mac, turn on "Bring the app to the foreground when a focus or break ends". The app then comes in front of whatever you are working in. If neither the main window nor the Mini Timer is open, the window you chose under "At launch, show" opens.

## Switch tasks during a Pomodoro

Start or continue another entry as usual. The phase carries on, because the Pomodoro belongs to you, not to the entry. Only stopping with nothing left running ends it.

## On the iPhone and iPad

The same button is on the Timer card: a tap acts as a click, and a long press opens the menu. A Pomodoro is local to its device, so one started on the Mac doesn't show on the phone. It survives closing the app there, and phase ends arrive as notifications.
