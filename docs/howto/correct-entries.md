# Correct your time

## Change an entry's times, task, or project

Click the row in the Timer, or double-click the card in the Calendar. In the popover, change a field and press Return. There is no Save button, and ⌘Z undoes the change.

```{figure} /images/mac-entry-popover.png
:alt: The edit popover open on an entry in the Timer, with the fields Task, Project, Start, Stop, Duration, and Date.

The same editor opens from the Timer, the Calendar, and Reports.
```

Changing Start keeps Stop where it is. Changing Stop or Duration keeps Start. To move an entry to another day with the same times, change Date.

## Fix the start of the running entry

Click the elapsed time in the Timer. Type the real start time into Start and press Return. The label jumps to the corrected duration, and the entry keeps running.

## Stop an entry you forgot

If a timer ran overnight, stop it with ⌘S first, because a running entry has no Stop to edit. Then open the entry and type its real length into Duration, which keeps Start, or press ⌘2 and drag the bottom edge of its card in the Calendar. For a small correction, typing a time into Stop works too: the app places that time on the date nearest the old stop.

An entry across dates is one entry, and you edit it from any of its rows.

## Add time you forgot to track

Press ⌘2 for the Calendar, find the day, and drag down in empty space in its Tracked column. Enter the task and project in the popover and press Return. Times snap to five minutes; hold ⌥ for single minutes. The default snap size is under Settings, Calendar.

If the tracker has recorded that stretch, [make the entry from the activity card](entries-from-activity.md) instead.

## Split or duplicate an entry

Right-click the card in the Calendar. Split Here cuts it at its midpoint, which you then adjust by dragging. Duplicate to Next Day (⌘D) copies it to the same time on the next day, as long as that time is past.

## Delete an entry

In the Timer, select the row and press ⌘⌫. In the Calendar, select the card and press Delete. Both can be undone with ⌘Z. To throw away the running entry, press ⌥⌘⌫.

## Resolve an overlap

In the Calendar, the header of a day with overlapping entries shows a ⚠ count. Click it to select those entries, then drag a card's edge until they no longer overlap, or leave them if the overlap is what happened.
